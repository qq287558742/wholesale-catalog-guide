Size Consistency Decides the Second Purchase

Published by DPJ Wholesale. If the second unit does not fit the first, the customer does not come back.

The rule

Consistency of fit is the single strongest predictor of a repeat purchase in our order data. Where the buyer could know the size before ordering, the line reorders; where they could not, the return rate rises and the economics get worse fast.

Where it shows up

  • Storage totes and replacement lids — the lid is the failure point, so retailers who stock lids hold the reorder on the tote itself.
  • Pet crate formats — size is dictated by breed, not fashion, so the right ladder reorders for years.
  • Apparel and footwear — the opposite. Fit is binary, returns are highest, and the economics are the worst in the catalog.
